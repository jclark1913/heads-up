# Share a vocabulary deck

1. Create and save a deck as usual.
2. Open **My decks → Share deck**, review the cards, then choose **Create share link**.
3. Show the QR for someone to scan, download the QR image, copy the link, or use **Share link** if your browser supports it.
4. The recipient previews the cards and chooses **Save a copy & play**. They can edit their copy like any other saved deck.

You can also paste a received link into **Open deck link** on Home.

No sign-in or backend is involved. Use the published GitHub Pages site when sharing between phones; a localhost link points to the recipient's own device, and temporary tunnel URLs expire.

## Copies and limits

The link contains the cards as they were when you shared them. Later edits do not update it. Anyone holding it can read or forward the cards, and it cannot be recalled.

Reopening the same shared content offers **Open saved copy** or **Save another copy**. Opening the existing copy preserves your edits. Saving another creates a separate local deck.

Large lists may exceed the QR limit. Copy the link instead, or select **Export words (.txt)** and send that file. Very large lists require a file or smaller decks. Recipients use **Create deck → Import file** and give the imported list a name. No words are dropped to make a QR fit.

Shared decks remain local to the recipient's browser after saving. Keep exported files or links as backups. The app still needs an online launch until offline caching is added.
